📋What is the DH Report?

The DH Report is the detailed transaction-level companion to the DH Analytics Dashboard. While the Analytics Dashboard gives you aggregate KPIs and charts, the DH Report lets you inspect every individual hedge entry in your Dynamic Hedging programme — its status, amounts, rates, and performance contribution.

💡 Key Value: Drill down into the individual entries that make up your portfolio. Understand which specific trades are performing well, which are consuming coverage, and how your bucket structure is being filled over time.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Entry-Level Transparency — every hedge entry with its creation date, value date, notional, currency pair, and execution rate
  • ✓
    Status Tracking — see the current status of each entry (open, closed, partially filled, expired)
  • ✓
    Performance Attribution — DH Performance, Pricing P&L, and Forward Points at the individual entry level
  • ✓
    Bucket Coverage — understand how entries are distributed across your defined hedging buckets
  • ✓
    Currency Pair Detail — analyse the programme currency pair by currency pair

📝Hedge Entries Table

The core of the DH Report is the entry-level data table. Each row represents a single hedge entry with the following key fields:

FieldDescription
Entry ReferenceUnique identifier for the hedge entry
Creation DateWhen the entry was created or submitted to the programme
Value DateThe settlement / maturity date of this entry
Currency PairThe FX pair (e.g. EUR/USD)
DirectionBuy (positive) or Sell (negative)
Notional AmountOriginal exposure amount in the entry's base currency
Normalised AmountNotional converted to your selected reporting currency
Hedge RateThe rate at which the entry was executed
StatusCurrent state of the entry
BucketThe hedging bucket or tenor band this entry belongs to
💡 Tip: Sort the table by Value Date to plan upcoming settlements, or sort by DH Performance to identify your best- and worst-performing entries.

📈Performance View

Alongside the entry details, the DH Report shows performance metrics at the individual entry level:

  • •
    DH Performance: How much value the Dynamic Hedging programme generated on this entry vs. an unhedged benchmark rate at the time of creation
  • •
    Pricing P&L: P&L from market rate movements between entry creation and today's valuation
  • •
    Forward Points: The cost-of-carry component embedded in the hedge rate — reflects the interest rate differential over the entry's tenor
🔍 Understanding Forward Points: Forward points can be positive or negative depending on the interest rate differential between the two currencies. A currency with a lower interest rate than EUR will typically have negative forward points on EUR buy trades (the forward rate is lower than spot).
⚠️ Open vs. Closed entries: Performance on open entries is indicative — it will continue to evolve until the entry matures and is cash-settled. Performance on closed entries is final and locked in.

🔄Data Refresh

  • 📅
    Data refreshes once per business day
  • 💱
    Normalisation rates and performance metrics are recalculated at each refresh
  • 📊
    New entries created today will appear after the next daily refresh
