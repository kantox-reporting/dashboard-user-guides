📐What is the DH Analytics Dashboard?

The DH Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your Dynamic Hedging programme. It lets you analyse outstanding FX exposure, understand the performance decomposition of your hedge portfolio, and monitor how your hedging activity evolves across currency pairs and value dates.

💡 Key Value: See your total outstanding exposure, its directional split, and the performance breakdown (pricing P&L, DH performance, and forward points) all in one view — with full multi-currency normalisation.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Exposure Overview — see total outstanding FX exposure by entity and currency pair
  • ✓
    Performance Decomposition — break down results into Pricing P&L, DH Performance, and Forward Points
  • ✓
    Directional Analysis — filter to positive (buy) or negative (sell) entries separately
  • ✓
    Multi-Currency Normalisation — view amounts in any supported currency, with automatic conversion at spot rates
  • ✓
    Creation vs. Value Date Views — switch between the date the hedge was created and the date it matures

🎛️Filters & Controls

FilterWhat it does
Entity GroupSelect the client entity to analyse
DirectionToggle between Positive Entries (buy side) and Negative Entries (sell side) — or leave unfiltered for both
Creation Date (Between)Filter by when the hedge entry was created
Value Date (Between)Filter by when the hedge matures and settles
Currency PairFilter to one or more currency pairs (e.g. EUR/USD, EUR/GBP)
Performance ParametersSelect which performance metric to display in the charts (DH Performance, Pricing P&L, Forward Points, etc.)
💡 Tip: The normalisation currency can be changed by request — switching between EUR and USD, for example, lets you review the same portfolio from different reporting perspectives without changing the underlying data.

📊Dashboard Visuals

🏢 Exposure Sent by Entity (Bar Chart)

A horizontal bar chart showing total FX volume traded, broken down by entity or account. Quickly identify which entities have the largest open exposure and whether any entity has a disproportionate share of the programme.

📊 KPI Cards

Performance %

Selected performance metric as a percentage — shows how the programme is performing relative to a benchmark

Exposure Saved

Value generated by the Dynamic Hedging programme relative to an unhedged benchmark

FX Volume Traded

Total normalised notional amount traded within the selected filters

🌊 Waterfall Chart — Exposure Details

The waterfall chart decomposes your exposure step by step:

  • 1
    Main Direction — the starting gross exposure on the dominant side
  • 2
    FX Volume Traded — total notional executed
  • 3
    Negative Amount — the sell-side (or buy-side if viewing the negative direction)
  • 4
    Netted Amount — net exposure after both sides are offset — this is your true outstanding risk
Reading the waterfall: Green bars increase the net exposure; red bars reduce it. The final bar (Netted Amount) shows your actual net position after netting positive and negative entries against each other.

🔖 Bookmark Navigator

Use the bookmark navigation buttons to switch between saved views — for example, switching between a creation-date perspective and a value-date perspective without adjusting filters manually.

📏Key Metrics Explained

MetricMeaning
FX Volume TradedTotal notional amount executed in the programme, normalised to your selected display currency
Positive AmountNotional on the buy (positive) side of your programme
Negative AmountNotional on the sell (negative) side
Netted AmountNet exposure — positive minus negative, showing your true outstanding risk
DH PerformanceValue created by the hedge relative to what the market rate was at the time of hedging — measures how well the programme timed its entries
Pricing P&LP&L from market rate movements between the entry creation date and the current valuation date
Forward PointsThe forward point component embedded in the hedge rate — reflects the interest rate differential between the two currencies over the tenor
🔍 Performance Parameters slicer: Select one metric at a time in the Performance Parameters slicer to see its contribution clearly. Combining DH Performance + Forward Points gives you the full cost of carry in the programme.

🔄Data Refresh

    Daily refresh — positions and entries reflect the previous business day
  • 💱

  • 📊
    Performance metrics are recalculated daily as market rates change
