🧭 What is K2 Bucket Exposure Monitoring?

Every hedging programme you run — a forecast of future flows, a committed (FirmCo) flow, or a BSI flow — is tracked here as a bucket: a self-contained unit of exposure with its own start date, currency pair, and hedging rule. This dashboard answers three questions for any bucket, at any level of zoom: how much exposure exists, how much of it has been hedged, and at what rate.

💡 Key value: Start at the top for a portfolio-wide read (total exposure, hedge progress, rates), then drill down bucket by bucket, and finally down to the individual trades behind any number that needs a closer look.

Key terms used throughout this guide

TermWhat it means
BucketOne hedging programme — a specific rule, currency pair, and start date. The unit everything else on this dashboard rolls up from.
BranchWhich kind of flow a bucket's exposure belongs to: Forecast (projected, not yet committed), FirmCo (firm/committed), or BSI.
TotalA bucket's real exposure — whichever is larger, the Forecast total or the combined FirmCo bookings, never both added together. See Exposure Breakdown.
Value DateThe settlement date the exposure is scheduled against. Stays constant across a bucket's underlying activity.
Execution Value DateThe date a specific underlying trade actually executed. Unlike Value Date, this can differ from one trade to the next within the same bucket.
WAHRWeighted Average Hedge Rate — the average rate already locked in through executed hedges.
WAERWeighted Average Entry Rate — the average rate at which the trades making up managed exposure were originally entered, weighted by amount.
Hedge RatioThe share of exposure that has already been hedged. Only meaningful at the Total level — see Bucket Table.
🌍 Display currency selector (top bar): Every monetary value on this dashboard — KPI cards, tables, and charts alike — can be viewed in your native transaction currency or converted to AUD, CHF, DKK, EUR, GBP, SEK, or USD. Switching it recalculates the whole page at once, so figures always stay comparable across currency pairs.

💶 General Exposure Breakdown

This is the portfolio-wide starting point: your total exposure, split into what's actively being managed and what's still fully open.

Total Exposure

€12.4M
The bucket's real exposure — whichever branch (Forecast, FirmCo, or BSI) is currently live for it. Not a sum of all three.

Managed Exposure

€9.8M
The portion of Total Exposure actively being handled by a hedging programme, split further by status below.

Open Exposure

€2.6M
The remaining, unmanaged portion of Total Exposure — not yet brought under a hedging programme.
Total Exposure = Managed Exposure + Open Exposure €12.4M = €9.8M + €2.6M
🔀 Why Total isn't Forecast + FirmCo + BSI: A bucket usually has one Forecast estimate and can have several FirmCo bookings as flows get firmed up over time. Total Exposure follows whichever is larger: the Forecast total, or all of those FirmCo bookings added together. Once cumulative FirmCo commitments overtake the original Forecast, Total switches to track FirmCo instead — but it's always one or the other, never both stacked on top of each other.

📈 Exposure Rate Evolution

This chart compares, for each market direction and currency pair, the rate already locked in through executed hedges (WAHR) against the average rate at which the trades behind managed exposure were originally entered (WAER). It's the fastest way to see how much the market has moved between when that exposure was created and when it was actually hedged.

DirectionCurrency PairWAHR (executed)WAER (entry)Reading
BuyEUR/USD1.08501.0910These trades were entered at a less favourable rate than what was ultimately hedged — the market moved in your favour before execution.
SellGBP/USD1.26401.2605These trades were entered at a more favourable rate than what was ultimately hedged — the market moved against you before execution.
💡 How to read it: WAHR and WAER sitting close together suggests the market was stable between entry and execution. A widening gap is worth a closer look at the Program KPI Table for that currency pair.

🪣 Bucket Table

One row per bucket, showing its exposure status and key KPIs. This table is expandable — open a row to see the branch breakdown behind that bucket's number, by value date.

🔀 Why Total is €1.9M, not €2.4M: Total follows whichever is larger — the Forecast total or all FirmCo bookings added together. Here Forecast (€1.9M) is still bigger than the combined FirmCo bookings (€0.3M + €0.2M = €0.5M), so Forecast drives Total. If those FirmCo bookings grew past €1.9M, Total would switch to track their sum instead. Either way, Total is never Forecast and FirmCo added together.
⚠️ Hedge Ratio and WAHR only apply to Total: hedges are executed against a bucket's Total Exposure, not against an individual branch, so Hedge Ratio and WAHR are only meaningful at the Total row. Branch rows exist purely to show composition, and don't carry their own Hedge Ratio or WAHR. A single branch can also show more than one value date, because a programme's activity can span several settlement dates at once — that's expected, not an error.

💡 Tips & Filter Context

  • •
    Currency is global. The top-bar currency selector affects every KPI, table, and chart on the page at once.
  • •
    Selections cascade downward. Picking a row in the Program KPI Table or Bucket Table narrows every visual below it to that selection.
  • •
    Numbers look narrower than expected? Check what's currently selected in the Program KPI Table or Bucket Table — that's almost always the cause.
  • •
    Total ≠ Forecast + FirmCo + BSI. This is expected — see Exposure Breakdown for why.
