📜What is the Standard Hedge Policy Dashboard?

The Standard Hedge Policy Dashboard provides a structured view of how your hedging activity aligns with your organisation's FX hedge policy. It shows actual coverage ratios per currency pair and time horizon alongside the total exposure and the policy targets defined in your programme, making it easy to identify compliance gaps or excesses.

💡 Key Value: See at a glance whether your hedging programme is operating within policy — which buckets are on target, which are under-hedged, and which exceed the upper policy limit.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Policy Target vs. Actual — compare actual hedge coverage ratios against your defined policy minimums and maximums
  • ✓
    Bin-Level Compliance — see policy status for each time bin (short, medium, long tenor)
  • ✓
    Currency Pair Breakdown — policy compliance by each hedged currency pair
  • ✓
    Trend View — how coverage ratios have evolved over time relative to policy
  • ✓
    Alert Indicators — visual signals when coverage falls outside policy bands

📊Coverage View

Coverage Ratio by Bin

Each bucket (time horizon) shows three values side by side:

  • 📌
    Policy minimum: The minimum hedge coverage ratio required by your policy for this bin (e.g. 40%)
  • 📌
    Policy maximum: The upper bound — hedging above this level is outside policy (e.g. 90%)
  • ▶
    Actual coverage: Your current hedge coverage ratio for this bucket, calculated as hedged notional ÷ gross exposure
Reading the chart: When the actual coverage bar falls between the policy min and max lines, you are in compliance. When it falls below the minimum, you are under-hedged. When it exceeds the maximum, you are over-hedged relative to policy.

Coverage Over Time

The trend view shows how coverage ratios have moved over your selected date range. A stable coverage ratio tracking within policy bands indicates a well-managed programme; sudden drops or spikes may indicate large exposure arrivals or hedge maturities that need attention.

✅Policy Compliance Summary

The compliance summary shows the overall status of each currency pair and bin combination:

StatusMeaning
✓ In PolicyActual coverage is within the defined minimum and maximum for this bin
↓ Under-HedgedCoverage is below the policy minimum — action may be required to add hedges
↑ Over-HedgedCoverage exceeds the policy maximum — review whether reduction or roll-forward is appropriate
⚠️ Policy is indicative: The policy targets shown in this dashboard reflect your programme configuration at the last data refresh. If your policy parameters have been updated, contact your Kantox programme manager to confirm the latest targets are reflected.

🔄Data Refresh

  • 📅
    Coverage and compliance data refreshes once per business day
  • 💱
    Exposure amounts are recalculated using the latest spot rates at each refresh
  • 📊
    New hedges executed today will appear in compliance calculations after the next daily refresh
