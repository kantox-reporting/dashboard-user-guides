📉What is the Mark-to-Market Dashboard?

The Mark-to-Market (MtM) Dashboard shows the current market value of your open FX hedges. It calculates the unrealised P&L on every open position by comparing your locked-in hedge rate against the current forward market rate at the same maturity. This tells you — in a multiple daily refresh scheme — whether your portfolio is "in the money" or "out of the money" relative to today's market.

💡 Key Value: See the theoretical gain or loss on your entire open portfolio at a glance, broken down by maturity month, with dynamic currency normalisation for multi-currency portfolios.

  • ✓

🌊MtM Valuation View

Waterfall Chart — P&L by Value Date

The main visual is a waterfall chart where each bar represents one period (month, quarter, or year depending on your date granularity setting):

  • 🟢
    Green bars — in-the-money positions for that maturity period. The current forward rate is more favourable than your locked hedge rate.
  • 🔴
    Red bars — out-of-the-money. The market moved against you since you placed the hedge.
  • ▶
    Cumulative total (last bar) — the overall portfolio P&L across all maturities
How MtM is calculated: For each trade, the dashboard compares your locked hedge rate vs. the current forward rate at that trade's exact maturity tenor (not a single spot rate). P&L = (Current forward rate − Hedge rate) × Notional amount. A positive result means the market moved in your favour.
⚠️ Important: MtM P&L is unrealised — it shows what your portfolio would be worth if all positions were closed today. Actual settlement amounts will differ as rates continue to move until the trade matures.

🏦Cash Settlements View

The dashboard also includes a cash flow and settlement view, showing amounts due per bank and per currency across value dates. This is useful for:

  • •
    Managing settlement concentration across your liquidity providers
  • •
    Planning liquidity for upcoming settlement dates
  • •
    Monitoring settlement amounts by currency pair

Toggle between the MtM view and the Cash Settlements view using the filter icon bookmark buttons at the top of the main page.

🔄Data Refresh

  • 📅
    MtM Dashboard refreshes multiple times a day adding new executed trades and taking into account the latest forward rates
  • 📊
    P&L figures are indicative and will fluctuate with market movements
