📈What is the Standard Historic MtM?

The Standard Historic MtM report shows how the mark-to-market value of your hedge portfolio has evolved over time. Unlike the "real-time" MtM Dashboard — which shows today's unrealised P&L — this report lets you look back and understand how your portfolio value has changed day by day, month by month, across the life of your hedging programme.

💡 Key Value: Understand the trajectory of your portfolio value over time. See how market movements have shifted your unrealised P&L, and identify periods where the portfolio was particularly in- or out-of-the-money relative to the prevailing market environment.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Historical P&L Trend — see how total portfolio MtM has moved day by day or month by month
  • ✓
    Rate History — compare your hedge rates against historical forward rates at each point in time
  • ✓
    Portfolio Composition Over Time — how the mix of in-the-money and out-of-the-money positions has shifted
  • ✓
    Period Comparison — compare MtM values across different reporting periods (month-end snapshots)
  • ✓
    Multi-Currency Normalisation — all historical values in your preferred reporting currency

📊Historical MtM View

Portfolio MtM Over Time

The main chart shows the total portfolio MtM value at each historical valuation date. Key elements to note:

  • 🟢
    Positive values (above zero) — the portfolio was in-the-money on that date: the current forward rates were more favourable than the locked hedge rates
  • 🔴
    Negative values (below zero) — the portfolio was out-of-the-money: market rates moved against the locked hedge rates
  • ◼
    Zero line — the break-even level where hedge rates equal prevailing market forward rates
Reading the trend: A portfolio that moves from negative to positive MtM over time means the market moved in your favour after hedging. Large swings indicate volatile market conditions during that period. Use this view to explain P&L movements to finance stakeholders.

Maturity Bucket Breakdown Over Time

The bucketed view shows how the MtM contribution from different maturity periods has changed. Short-dated buckets (near-term maturities) may behave differently from long-dated buckets as rates evolve.

⚖️Hedge Rate vs. Market Rate Comparison

The rate comparison view shows, for each historical date:

  • —
    Locked hedge rate: The weighted average hedge rate of your open portfolio on that date
  • —
    Market forward rate: The prevailing forward rate at the corresponding maturity on that date
  • ▲
    Rate differential: The gap between the two lines — when the hedge rate is above the market forward rate on a sell position, you are out-of-the-money
🔍 Using this for review: When presenting to your treasury committee or auditors, use this chart to demonstrate how the MtM values correspond to actual rate movements — it shows the P&L is a function of market dynamics, not programme error.
⚠️ Historical MtM is indicative: Past MtM values shown in this report were unrealised at the time. The actual P&L realised at settlement may differ from the MtM shown for any given period, as rates continued to move between the valuation date and the trade's maturity.

🔄Data Refresh

  • 📅
    Historical MtM values are calculated and stored daily — each business day a new data point is added to the history
  • 🗓️
    Yesterday's MtM snapshot becomes available after today's daily refresh
  • 💱
    Historical normalised values use the spot rate that was in effect on each historical date — not today's rate
💡 Historical accuracy: Because each data point captures the rates as of that specific date, the historical series is stable — past values will not change retroactively when today's rates move.
