📊 What is Entry Detailed Analysis?

This page breaks a position down to its individual entries — every trade booked against that position, seen separately rather than netted together. It answers the question "what exactly makes up this exposure, and where is the PnL coming from?" by showing the same numbers two ways: netted (the position's real committed exposure) and unnetted (every entry counted on its own).

💡 Key value: Use this page when a summary number elsewhere doesn't add up the way you expect, or when you need to see exactly which entries, cancellations, or hedge relationships are driving a position's PnL.
🔍 Top KPI ribbon: The six tiles across the top of this page (Total Unnetted Exposure, Total Netted Exposure, Managed Netted Exposure, FX Volume Saved, Total PnL, Forward Point Savings) are the same on every page of this report — see the Exposure Overview guide for what they mean.

💹 PnL Breakdown by Type

The PnL breakdown card splits total PnL into five components, and shows each one three ways — Gross, Net, and (CR):

Gross = Net + (CR)
💡 Cancellation Rate: Some clients configure a Cancellation Rate — a percentage of each entry's amount that's reserved and deliberately held back from hedging, from the moment the entry is booked. That reserved percentage is the Unmanaged amount; the rest is Managed. Both can exist at the same time on the same entry. When an entry's Cancellation Rate is 0%, there's no unmanaged portion at all, so Gross and Net come out equal.

📗 Gross

PnL across the entry's full amount — Managed plus Unmanaged.

📘 Net

PnL on the Managed portion only — the part actually being hedged.

📙 (CR)

PnL on the Unmanaged portion only — the part held back by the Cancellation Rate setting.

The Five PnL Types

PnL Type Description Calculation example
Pricing PnL The pricing markup captured at trade inception — the difference between the rate the entry was priced at and the live mid-market spot rate at that same moment. Priced at 1.1050, mid-market spot at creation 1.1030, on 100,000 → 100,000 × (1.1050 − 1.1030) = 200
Spot Realized PnL For entries that have closed, netted, cancelled, or expired: the actual profit/loss from spot-rate movement between execution and closing. Historic spot rate at closing 1.1080 vs. the rate hedged at execution 1.1050, on 100,000 (buy) → 100,000 × (1.1080 − 1.1050) = 300
Spot Unrealized PnL For entries still open: the mark-to-market gain/loss from spot movement since creation — not yet realized. Spot mid-rate at creation 1.1030 vs. current live spot 1.1000, on 100,000 (buy) → 100,000 × (1.1030 − 1.1000) = 300
Fwd Realized PnL For closed entries: the realized gain/loss from the forward-points (interest-rate differential) component of the hedge. Hedge executed at spot 1.1050 against a contracted forward rate; realized forward-point pickup on 100,000 → 80
Fwd Unrealized PnL For entries still open: the mark-to-market forward-points gain/loss versus the live forward rate. Live forward rate moved slightly against the position since creation, on 100,000 → −20
Total PnL Sum of the five types above. 200 + 300 + 300 + 80 − 20 = 860
💡 Note: All figures are normalized to your report's selected display currency before being summed, so PnL across entries in different currency pairs can be safely added together.

📈 PnL Value Distribution Chart

The bar chart shows the same Gross / Net / (CR) split as the PnL breakdown card, but with the (CR) portion further divided by whether the underlying entry's value date is still ahead of us or already past:

  • •
    Gross — total PnL including everything ever booked (same as Gross above)
  • •
    Net — PnL on what's still actively managed today
  • •
    Open (CR) — Unmanaged PnL where the entry's value date hasn't arrived yet
  • •
    Expired (CR) — Unmanaged PnL where the entry's value date has already passed

Bars are also colour-broken-down by entry status (Closed - Executed, Canceled, Expired, Monitoring, Expired (CR), Open (CR)), so you can see which workflow states are contributing to each total.

🔗 Filter Context

This page carries several filters inherited from wherever you drilled through from — client, create date, value date, currency pair, and workflow status among them. They aren't shown as visible controls on this page; they're applied automatically based on the entry, order, or position you drilled through on.

💡 Tip: If the numbers on this page look narrower than you expected, check what you originally clicked on to drill through — that's almost always what's filtering the page.
