🧭 What is PnL Overview?

This page breaks Total PnL down as a visual tree — showing how the two big drivers (pricing and spot movement vs. forward-point impact) roll up into the total — and then lets you pick any one PnL type to explore by currency pair and over time.

🔍 Top KPI ribbon: The six tiles across the top of this page are the same on every page of this report — see the Exposure Overview guide for what they mean.

🧭 Related Pages

The report's top navigation only shows three tabs: Exposure Overview, Forward Point Savings, and this page. Everything else lives one level deeper, reached from a panel of buttons on the right edge of this page:

PageWhat it adds
PnL by Status The same PnL, broken out by currency pair and by month instead of the tree/picker view here.
Position Detailed Analysis PnL and exposure at the individual position level — one row per position, with its own path down to entry-level detail.
💡 Tip: Think of these three pages as one connected view of PnL at increasing granularity — overview here, then by pair/month, then position-by-position. The same side panel appears on all three so you can move between them without going back to the top navigation.

🌳 PnL Breakdown

A set of connected cards shows how Total PnL rolls up from its five components:

Total PnL ├── Pricing + Spot PnL │ ├── Pricing PnL │ └── Spot PnL (Spot Realized + Spot Unrealized) └── Forward Point Impact └── Forward Points PnL (Fwd Realized + Fwd Unrealized)
💡 Reading the cards: Each card shows its PnL value alongside that value as a percentage of your total unnetted exposure — a quick read on how much of your trading volume that PnL component represents.

📐 How Each PnL Type Is Calculated

Each of the five PnL types only applies in a specific situation, and compares a different pair of rates. This is the general logic behind each one — for the Gross / Net / (CR) breakdown of any of them, see the Entry Detailed Analysis guide.

PnL Type When it applies Rates compared
Pricing PnL Every entry, regardless of status The rate the entry was priced at vs. the mid-market spot rate at the moment it was created — the pricing markup built in at inception.
Spot Realized PnL Only once an entry has closed, netted, been cancelled, or expired The historic spot rate on the day it closed vs. the rate it was actually hedged at (if closed/netted), or vs. its valuation rate at that time (if cancelled/expired).
Spot Unrealized PnL Only while an entry is still open with a future value date The mid-market spot rate at creation vs. today's live spot rate — a mark-to-market snapshot, not yet realized.
Fwd Point Impact Realized Only once an entry has closed or netted The spot rate at hedge execution vs. the contracted hedge (forward) rate — the forward-points value locked in by hedging.
Fwd Point Impact Unrealized Only while an entry is still open with a future value date Today's live spot rate vs. today's live forward rate — the mark-to-market forward-points movement on a trade that hasn't closed yet.
⚠️ Note: A PnL type shows as zero outside its applicable situation — e.g. Spot Realized PnL is 0 for an entry that's still open, since nothing has closed yet to realize.

🎛️ Interactive Picker

A button list lets you pick any one of the five PnL types (Pricing PnL, Spot Realized PnL, Spot Unrealized PnL, or either Forward Points PnL variant) to drive two charts:

ChartWhat it shows
By currency pair A bar chart of your selected PnL type, one bar per directional currency pair.
Evolution by PnL state A stacked column and line chart of your selected PnL type by month, split by Realized vs. Unrealized.
💡 Tip: The three summary cards above (Pricing PnL, Spot PnL, Forward Points PnL) always show their own fixed totals regardless of what you pick here — only the two charts below the picker respond to your selection.
