📊 What is Exposure Overview?

This is the landing page of the report — a health check of your total netted FX exposure, sliced four different ways: by status, by currency, by currency pair, and over time. The same underlying number (Total Netted Exposure) runs through every chart on this page; each visual just cuts it along a different dimension.

💡 Key value: Start here to get your bearings — which currencies and pairs carry the most exposure, how much sits in each status, and how that total has moved over time — then use the other pages, or drill through into a specific entry or position, for detail.
🧭 Navigation: The tabs at the top of the report are Exposure Overview, Forward Point Savings, and PnL Overview — everything else (PnL by Status, Position Detailed Analysis, Entry Detailed Analysis) is reached from within PnL Overview. See that page's guide for the full picture.

🧮 Top KPI Ribbon

The six tiles across the top of this page appear identically on every page of the report:

Metric Description Calculation example
Total Unnetted Exposure The sum of every individual entry's notional amount, added up one by one — no netting between entries that offset each other. A position has a buy entry of 1,000,000 and a sell entry of 400,000 → 1,000,000 + 400,000 = 1,400,000
Total Netted Exposure Entries that share the same standardized currency pair but face opposite directions net against each other — a buy counts as positive, a sell as negative — plus any amount still held back by a client's Cancellation Rate setting. The same buy of 1,000,000 and sell of 400,000, same standardized pair → 1,000,000 − 400,000 = 600,000 net buy
Managed Netted Exposure The position's core net-by-direction amount only, excluding any amount held back by a Cancellation Rate setting. Same position → 600,000 net buy
FX Volume Saved The FX volume avoided by netting entries at the position level instead of trading each one individually. Gross entry volume 1,400,000 minus the actual net position volume 600,000 → 1,400,000 − 600,000 = 800,000 saved
Total PnL Sum of five PnL components — Pricing, Spot Realized, Spot Unrealized, Fwd Realized, and Fwd Unrealized. 200 + 300 + 300 + 80 − 20 = 860
Forward Point Savings Value gained from the forward-point saving mechanism — the difference between the forward rate and spot rate captured at entry vs. at trigger, applied to each entry's notional. An entry saved 45 forward points on a 100,000 notional trade, contributing 450; summed across all entries in the period → 3,200
🔍 Unnetted vs. netted: "Unnetted" counts every entry on its own — useful for total trading activity. "Netted" reflects what's actually still open once entries in opposite directions on the same pair cancel each other out — this is the number that matters for real exposure and hedging decisions, and it's what every chart below is built from.

🔧 Filters

Click the filter icon in the top-right corner to open a filter panel over the page, with a Close button to dismiss it. It offers:

  • •
    Create Date and Value Date — restrict to entries booked or settling in a date range
  • •
    Entity Group and Company Name — restrict to a client or group of related clients
  • •
    Currency Pair — restrict to one or more directional currency pairs
  • •
    Standardized Status — restrict to specific statuses (e.g. only open positions)
  • •
    Position Reference and Order Reference — jump straight to a known reference

🌳 Total Netted Exposure Status Breakdown

A drill-down tree that splits Total Netted Exposure first into broad groups, then into the specific status each slice sits in (e.g. Closed - Executed, Monitoring, Canceled, Expired). Click into a branch to see how much of the total belongs to that group, then keep drilling to the individual status level.

💡 Tip: This is the fastest way to answer "how much of our exposure is still genuinely open versus already closed or wound down?"

💱 Total Netted Exposure by Currency

A column chart with one bar per currency, each bar split into colored segments by status. Use it to see which currencies carry the most exposure, and whether that exposure is mostly still open or already closed out.

📈 Total Netted Exposure Evolution

A combo chart showing Total Netted Exposure by month, stacked and colored by status, with two rate lines overlaid so you can compare them directly: WAER and WAHR.

📊 Stacked columns

Total Netted Exposure per month, broken down by status — read the trend of how much exposure builds up or winds down over time.

📉 WAER

Weighted average entry rate — the position's running average rate at creation.

📉 WAHR

Weighted average hedge rate — the actual rate positions were hedged at.

💡 Why both: Showing WAER and WAHR side by side makes it easy to compare where you entered against where you actually hedged — useful for judging execution performance.
⚠️ Note: The rate lines only appear when a single currency pair is selected in the filter panel — averaging a rate across different currency pairs wouldn't be meaningful.

🔀 Exposure by Currency Pair

A bar chart with one pair of bars per directional currency pair (e.g. EUR/USD, GBP/USD) — Unnetted Exposure next to Netted Exposure for each. Comparing the two side by side shows exactly how much netting is reducing your exposure, pair by pair.
