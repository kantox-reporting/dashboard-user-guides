💳What is the Cash Settlements Report?

The Cash Settlements report provides a granular view of actual cash amounts due across your open FX positions. Rather than showing unrealised P&L (as the MtM Dashboard does), this report focuses on the real money movement — what you will pay or receive at each settlement date, broken down by bank counterparty and currency pair.

💡 Key Value: Know exactly how much cash you need to settle with each bank, on each value date, in each currency. Essential for daily liquidity management and settlement risk monitoring.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Bank-level Settlement View — see total obligations due per liquidity provider (bank) across value dates
  • ✓
    Currency-level Detail — understand the currency breakdown of your settlement obligations
  • ✓
    Concentration Monitoring — identify if settlement amounts are concentrated with a single counterparty or date
  • ✓
    Timeline View — filter by value date range to focus on imminent settlements or longer-term obligations

🏛️Settlement by Bank

The bank-level view shows your settlement obligations grouped by liquidity provider — each row (or bar segment) represents one bank:

  • •
    Total normalised amount due to or from each bank on each value date
  • •
    Number of outstanding trades per bank
  • •
    Distribution across value dates — spikes indicate concentrated settlement risk
⚠️ Settlement concentration: If a large proportion of your settlement obligations fall on a single date with a single bank, this represents concentration risk. Use this view to monitor and manage this proactively with your treasury team.

💱Settlement by Currency

The currency view breaks down the same settlement obligations by currency pair, giving you:

  • •
    Total buy-side amount per currency (what you will receive)
  • •
    Total sell-side amount per currency (what you will pay out)
  • •
    Net settlement position per currency pair across the selected period
🔍 Netting opportunities: If you have both buy and sell trades in the same currency pair settling on the same date with the same bank, these may be eligible for netting. Confirm with your bank counterparty whether bilateral netting is in place for your account.

🔄Data Refresh

  • 📅
    Settlement data refreshes six times a day
  • 💱
    Normalised amounts use the most recent available spot rates at each refresh
⚠️ Note: Cash settlement amounts shown here are based on the originally agreed hedge rate and notional — they do not change with market movements (unlike the MtM P&L). However, normalised totals may shift slightly as the spot conversion rate used for display updates daily.
