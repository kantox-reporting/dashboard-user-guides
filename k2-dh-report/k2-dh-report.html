📋What is the K2 DH Report?

The K2 DH Report is the traceability tool for the K2 Dynamic Hedging programme. Its core purpose is to let you follow every exposure entry from submission to coverage — showing exactly which hedge or trade covered each entry, at what rate, and in what status.

The report presents a single flexible table with two selectable field groups: Entries (submission and booking detail) and Coverage (the hedge or trade that covered each entry). You choose which fields to display using the field selector panel on the left side of the report.

💡 Key Value: Answer the question "what happened to this exposure?" — trace any entry through its full lifecycle, from the moment it was submitted to the programme to the moment it was covered by a trade, along with the rate and entity that executed the cover.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Full Traceability — follow each exposure entry from submission through coverage to execution, including the entity reference of the covering trade
  • ✓
    Selectable Field Groups — switch between Entries-focused and Coverage-focused views in the same table using the field selector
  • ✓
    Bucket-Aware View — exposure is grouped by bucket, reflecting the K2 programme's date-range grouping of exposures
  • ✓
    Coverage Metrics — see the covered ratio, managed and open amounts, and EUM % for each entry or group of entries
  • ✓
    WAHR at Entry Level — the Weighted Average Hedge Rate broken down by spot and forward components for each covered entry
⚠️ One entry, multiple coverage rows: An entry can have more than one coverage row — for example, if it is partially executed in multiple trades. When you want to see entry-level totals without duplication, deselect all Coverage group fields from the field selector.

🪣K2 & Buckets

The K2 programme introduces the concept of buckets — date ranges that group together exposures with different value dates. Instead of requiring every exposure to match an exact hedge value date, clients submit entries into a bucket, and the programme manages hedging at the bucket level.

  • •
    Bucket grouping — exposures with value dates within the same date range are automatically combined into one bucket for hedging purposes
  • •
    Flexible value dates — clients do not need to match the exact value date of a hedge; entries within the bucket window are combined regardless of their specific date
  • •
    Traceability within buckets — when new entries arrive in a bucket, the report helps determine whether existing hedges already cover the new exposure or whether additional hedging is needed
  • •
    Hedge rate per entry — even though hedging is managed at bucket level, traceability assigns a hedge rate to each individual entry, giving you a per-entry view of the programme's performance
🔍 Buckets vs. standard DH: Buckets are a K2-specific feature. In the standard DH programme (K1), each exposure entry is matched to a specific value date. In K2, entries are grouped by date range into buckets, reducing the operational overhead of exact date matching.

📝Entries Fields

The Entries field group contains the submission and booking detail for each exposure entry. These fields describe what was sent to the programme, not how it was covered. Select these fields when you want to review entry volumes, dates, and amounts without the repetition that coverage rows can introduce.

🔗Coverage Fields

The Coverage field group shows how each entry was covered — which trade, at what rate, and with what status. One entry can have multiple coverage rows if it was covered in stages. These fields are relevant when you want to trace the specific hedge that covered an entry.

FieldDescription
Entity TypeWhether the covering entity is an internal trade or an external (bank) trade
Entity ReferenceThe reference of the trade that covered this entry (e.g. NSF-ZYL4T13W for external, K-0ABPQ37FM for internal). Swap legs appear with a suffix (e.g. NSF-X7GC9BMY-01).
Entity StatusStatus of the covering trade (e.g. Executed, In_position, Cancelled, Expired)
Entity Execution DateWhen the covering trade was executed in the market
Entity Market DirectionBuy or sell direction of the covering trade
Entity Amount / Counter AmountNotional of the covering trade in base and counter currency
Entity Exchange RateThe rate of the covering trade
Entity Value DateSettlement date of the covering trade
Cover TypeThe type of cover applied to this entry
Cover IDIdentifier of the cover record
Covered Amount / Counter AmountThe portion of the entry's notional that this coverage row accounts for
Liquidity ProviderThe bank or counterparty that executed the external trade
Covered RatioPercentage of the entry's exposure that is covered by this coverage row
⚠️ Statuses other than Executed: Coverage rows with status In_position, Cancelled, or Expired count as managed exposure but will not have a full set of execution fields populated (e.g. Entity Exchange Rate will be blank). This is expected behaviour.
💡 Swap legs: If the Entity Reference shows a suffix like -01, this coverage row corresponds to a swap leg. The base reference without the suffix is the original trade.

📈Key Metrics

The summary KPIs shown at the top of the report give a quick overview of the programme's coverage state for the selected filters:

MetricMeaning
Amount ValueTotal notional of the selected entries in the entry's base currency
Open Exposure ValuePortion of the entry notional that has not yet been covered by any hedge
Managed Exposure ValuePortion of the entry notional under active coverage (including in-position, executed, or monitoring statuses)
Covered ValuePortion of the entry notional fully covered by executed trades
In Position ValuePortion of the entry notional where coverage exists but the trade has not yet been executed
Expired ValuePortion of the entry notional where coverage expired before execution
Covered RatioCovered Value ÷ Total Amount Value — the percentage of exposure that has been fully executed
EUM %Exposure Under Management — the percentage of total exposure that is actively managed by the programme (covered + in-position)
🔍 EUM % vs Covered Ratio: EUM % includes all managed exposure (even if not yet executed), while Covered Ratio counts only entries where execution has actually completed. EUM % ≥ Covered Ratio at all times.
