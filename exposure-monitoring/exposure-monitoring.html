🎯What is the Exposure Monitoring Dashboard?

The Exposure Monitoring Dashboard provides a consolidated view of your total FX exposure and how much of it is currently hedged. It helps you track your coverage position across currency pairs and time horizons, so you always know how much exposure remains open and unprotected at any point.

💡 Key Value: Keep a real-time pulse on your hedging coverage — see at a glance which currency pairs are under-hedged, over-hedged, or on target, and act before exposure gaps become costly.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Total Exposure Overview — gross FX exposure by currency pair and by time bucket
  • ✓
    Hedge Coverage Tracking — how much of your gross exposure has been hedged at each maturity horizon
  • ✓
    Residual Exposure — the open, unhedged portion of your FX risk in each currency pair
  • ✓
    Coverage Ratio KPIs — percentage hedged vs. total exposure, compared against your policy target
  • ✓
    Multi-Currency View — all amounts normalised to your preferred reporting currency

📊Exposure View

Gross Exposure by Currency Pair

The main chart shows your total gross FX exposure broken down by currency pair. Each bar (or segment) represents the full amount of FX risk in that pair — both the portion that has been hedged and the open remainder.

  • 🟦
    Hedged amount — the portion of exposure covered by open hedge positions
  • 🟥
    Residual / unhedged amount — the open exposure yet to be covered
Reading the chart: A bar that is mostly hedged (blue) indicates good coverage. A bar dominated by the unhedged segment (red) signals that you may want to add hedges for that currency pair before rates move further.

Exposure by Bucket / Horizon

The bucketed view groups exposure by time horizon (e.g. 0–1 month, 1–3 months, 3–6 months). This helps you identify whether your coverage is well distributed across tenors or concentrated in the near term.

⚠️ Near-term buckets: Exposure in the 0–1 month bucket is the most urgent to hedge. If the near-term bucket shows significant unhedged amounts, prioritise these with your treasury team.

📏Coverage Ratios

The coverage ratio KPIs show your hedge coverage as a percentage of gross exposure, per currency pair and in aggregate:

MetricMeaning
Coverage %Hedged amount ÷ Gross exposure — the proportion of your total FX risk that is covered
Gross ExposureTotal FX amount (hedged + unhedged) in the selected currency pair and period
Hedged AmountNotional of open hedge positions covering the selected exposure
Residual ExposureGross Exposure minus Hedged Amount — your current open risk
💡 Policy targets: Your Dynamic Hedging programme defines coverage ratio targets for each bucket and currency pair. Compare the Coverage % shown here against your policy targets to identify breaches or over-hedging situations.

🔄Data Refresh

  • 📅
    Exposure data refreshes once per business day
  • 💱
    Normalised amounts use the latest available spot rates at each refresh
  • 📊
    New hedges executed today will be reflected after the next daily refresh
⚠️ Intraday changes: If you execute new hedges during the day, coverage ratios will not update until the next refresh. For real-time confirmation, verify new trades directly in the Kantox platform.
