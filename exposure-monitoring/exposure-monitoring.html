🎯What is the Exposure Monitoring Dashboard?

The Exposure Monitoring Dashboard gives you a joint view of your FX exposure entries and the Dynamic Hedging positions covering them. Rather than showing only a single coverage ratio, it lets you see the full picture: what you have submitted to the programme, what positions exist against that exposure, and how those positions are distributed across their lifecycle statuses.

💡 Key Value: Understand at a glance how your submitted exposure relates to active DH positions — broken down by currency pair, status, and date — so you can spot gaps, expiring positions, or positions in monitoring before they affect your programme.

Key Benefits

  • ✓
    Positions & Entries Together — see your DH positions (hedges) and exposure entries side by side in a linked, expandable table
  • ✓
    Status Breakdown — understand how much of your position notional is Open, Monitoring, Expired, or Closed at any point in time
  • ✓
    WAHR Tracking — the Weighted Average Hedge Rate (WAHR) is shown at position level, giving you a blended rate view across your portfolio
  • ✓
    Normalised Amounts — all amounts can be viewed in a common reporting currency for cross-pair comparison
  • ✓
    Expand / Collapse — drill from an aggregated position view down into the individual entries linked to each position

📊Positions & Entries View

The main view of the dashboard shows DH positions and their linked exposure entries in an expandable hierarchy. The ▼ button expands a position to reveal the individual entries assigned to it; the ▲ button collapses them back.

Position-Level Information

Each position row shows the DH hedge that was opened to cover exposure. Key fields at this level:

FieldDescription
Position ReferenceUnique identifier for the DH hedge position
Creation DateWhen the position was opened in the programme
Value DateThe settlement / maturity date of the position
Currency PairFX pair hedged by this position
DirectionBuy or sell
Original AmountNotional amount of the position in its base currency
Normalised AmountNotional converted to your selected reporting currency
WAHRWeighted Average Hedge Rate — the blended rate at which this position is hedged
StatusCurrent lifecycle state of the position (Open, Monitoring, Expired, Closed, Cancelled)
🔍 What is WAHR? The Weighted Average Hedge Rate (WAHR) is the blended execution rate across all trades within a position, weighted by notional amount. It is the effective rate at which your DH programme has hedged this exposure.

Entry-Level Information

Expanding a position reveals the individual exposure entries linked to it. Each entry row shows the original FX exposure record submitted to the programme, including its creation date, amount, currency, value date, and status.

💡 Tip: Use the expand view to verify which of your submitted entries are covered by a specific position. Entries that appear without a linked position may still be waiting for coverage.

📏Exposure by Status

The Exposure by Status page provides an aggregated breakdown of your total DH position notional grouped by lifecycle status. This gives you a quick health check on the state of your hedging portfolio:

StatusMeaning
OpenThe position is active and hedging live exposure. It has not yet reached its value date.
MonitoringThe position is in an active monitoring phase — the programme is watching market conditions but the position has not yet executed or expired.
ExpiredThe position has passed its value date without executing. The related exposure is no longer covered by this position.
ClosedThe position has been fully executed and settled. The hedging cycle for this position is complete.
CancelledThe position was cancelled before execution. The related exposure may remain unhedged.
⚠️ Monitoring expired positions: A significant notional in Expired status may indicate that exposure was not covered before its value date. Review these positions with your Kantox account manager to understand whether the exposure was settled unhedged or rolled forward.
💡 Monitoring status: Positions in Monitoring status are not yet executed but remain active in the programme. Their final outcome (closed vs. expired) depends on whether a suitable market opportunity is found before the value date.

🔄Data Refresh

  • 📅
    Exposure and position data refreshes once per business day
  • 💱
    WAHR and normalised amounts are recalculated at each refresh using latest spot rates
  • 📊
    New entries and positions created today will appear after the next daily refresh
⚠️ Intraday changes: Positions executed or entries created during the day will not be reflected until the next refresh. For real-time trade confirmation, verify activity directly in the Kantox platform.
